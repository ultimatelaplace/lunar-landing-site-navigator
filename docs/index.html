Compare landing sites and dates at the Moon's south pole: where the Sun keeps a lander powered and where Earth stays in view for direct communication.

Accuracy

  • Sun and Earth directions match JPL Horizons within 0.00003° (40 checks at 4 sites on 5 dates).
  • The terrain map is read without a pixel shift (checked against Shackleton crater and against a second LOLA map).
  • Sampling the horizon twice as finely changes the share of sunlight by at most 0.17 percentage points and the longest darkness at the suggested sites by at most 1.5 hours.
  • The Moon's atmosphere is ignored for refraction: its surface pressure is about 3 × 10⁻¹⁵ bar (NASA Moon Fact Sheet).
  • Limits: the terrain map has 20 m pixels, and results use 10-minute steps, so event times can be off by up to 10 minutes. Full details are in ACCURACY.md in the source code.
