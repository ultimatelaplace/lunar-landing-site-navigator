The problem

The south pole is the hardest place to land

  • Near the equator, Earth stays in view and the Sun is seen for longer.
  • At the south pole, ridges and craters block the low Sun and Earth: no power, no radio contact, freezing cold.
  • A lander gets its power from solar panels and needs Earth in view to talk to us.
  • So choosing correct, error-free landing coordinates and dates is critical.

What Triad does

Best sites, best dates, alternatives in seconds

  • Checks 26,045 places from 84°S to the pole, every 10 minutes through 2027–2028.
  • Suggests the 10 best sites and a 30-day landing window.
  • Shows the best point in each of NASA's Artemis III regions.
  • If a site can't be used, finds alternatives from 50 m away.
  • Your own coordinates, and a printable report.

How it works

NASA data in, clear answers out

1 · TerrainNASA LRO LOLA south pole map, 20 m pixels. From each place we look out 150 km in 360 directions to find the horizon.
2 · Sun and EarthNAIF SPICE (NASA/JPL) gives their directions every 10 minutes, including the Moon's wobble (libration).
3 · VisibilityShare of the Sun's disk above the terrain (power), and Earth's whole disk in view (radio).
4 · RankingTeam rule: Earth in view ≥ 30% of the time, then the shortest darkness, then the most sunlight.

Checked against NASA JPL Horizons, NASA Moon Trek and LROC QuickMap.

What we found

Small moves, huge differences

66% → 21%Moving only 50 m from suggested site 2 can cut the sunlight from 66% to 21% and stretch the longest darkness from 9 to 147 days (2027).
4 of 10of Triad's suggested sites lie inside NASA's Artemis III candidate regions, although Triad did not look at the regions when choosing them.

Can you trust it?

Checked, measured, tested

  • Sun and Earth directions match NASA JPL Horizons within 0.00003° (40 checks).
  • Terrain map read without a pixel shift (Shackleton crater, second LOLA map).
  • Sampling twice as finely changes the sunlight by at most 0.17 percentage points.
  • A first-time user did all 7 test tasks without help. All 10 sites checked by the team in LROC QuickMap.
